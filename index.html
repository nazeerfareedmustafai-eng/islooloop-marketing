Frequently asked questions

A few things you may be wondering.

Is Islooloop free?

Yes, Islooloop is free to use.

Why should I use Islooloop?

Use Islooloop to share thoughts and discover local conversations without making your identity the focus. You stay in control of what you share and whether to accept private conversations.

Will my personal information be safe?

Islooloop is designed with privacy in mind: your email is not public, your identity is hidden by default, and location is shown at city level. You can review the privacy details on the official website.

Do I have to use my real name or photo?

No. You can share anonymously by default, and your name and photo stay hidden unless you choose otherwise.

Can someone message me without my permission?

Private conversations are consent-first: a chat opens only if you accept an invitation.

Can I report something or delete my data?

Reports are handled confidentially, and the official site says you can delete your data at any time. Visit islooloop.com for details.
